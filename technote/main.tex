\title{Signal Models in CCDarkSens: \\[2pt]
\large QEDark/QCDark2 DM--Electron Scattering, Dark Photon Absorption, \\
and the Migdal Effect — Standard Silicon and \SrCdSb{} Projections}
\author{Diego Venegas-Vargas}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
CCDarkSens is a general dark matter sensitivity framework for DAMIC-M,
built around a detector-response and profile-likelihood-ratio (PLR)
pipeline that is agnostic to the physical process generating the
deposited-energy spectrum $dR/dE$. This technote documents its signal
channels: DM--electron scattering, dark photon (hidden photon) absorption,
the Migdal effect, and elastic WIMP--nucleus scattering. For the first
three, it derives the rate formulas from first principles, presents the
standard silicon benchmark case, and gives the narrow-gap \SrCdSb{}
(``HypMat'') projection case with explicit, itemized assumptions. It also
derives the shared charge-ionization model (Klein's formula and the
$P(n_e\mid E)$ table) and documents a numerical-binning correctness issue
specific to the dark photon channel's monochromatic signal. This document
is a LaTeX rendering of \texttt{docs/DM\_Signal\_Models\_Physics\_Reference.md}
in the CCDarkSens repository, intended as source material for a
collaboration-facing technote.
\end{abstract}
